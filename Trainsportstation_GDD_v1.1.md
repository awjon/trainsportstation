# Trainsportstation GDD v1.1

## Vision
A whimsical real-time 3D railroad puzzle game built with Three.js and Kenney assets. Players have 10–15 seconds to build before trains automatically depart.

## Pillars
- Nintendo-style accessibility
- Fun-first arcade physics
- Build → Watch → Retry
- Multiple valid solutions
- Data-driven content
- Community scenarios

## Core Loop
Preview → Build countdown → Speed bet → Dispatch → Watch → Score (Connection) → Retry.

## Mechanics
Limited pieces: straight, curve, bridge, ramp, tunnel, junction, crossing, station.
One train to four trains across progression.
Arcade physics with momentum, jumps, gravity and derail limits.

## Progression
10 biomes. One new mechanic per world.

## Scoring
Connection is progression.
Stars:
1 Complete
2 Efficient build
3 Bonus speed/time
Bonuses: optional stations, unused pieces, no crashes, speed multiplier.

## Lore
The Guild of Conductors reconnects humanity after the AI era left communities isolated.

## Editor
Gameplay tools == editor tools.
Scenarios are JSON.

## Out of Scope
Economy, multiplayer, weather physics, procedural generation for v1.
